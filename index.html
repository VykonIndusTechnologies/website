09 — FAQ

Technical FAQ

Common questions regarding solar PV implementation timelines, BESS architectures, and operational warranties.

How long does a commercial solar installation take?

For most rooftop commercial systems (100kWp to 500kWp), physical construction on-site takes between 3 to 6 weeks. However, the end-to-end cycle including site structural evaluation, custom design approvals, procurement, utility net-metering documentation, and synchronization can span 8 to 12 weeks. We handle the entire engineering and regulatory pipeline.

What maintenance do solar panel arrays require?

Solar panels are highly reliable with no moving parts. The main recurring task is module cleaning (soiling removal) to prevent hotspot degradation and maintain efficiency, usually scheduled bi-weekly. We offer AMC packages that cover periodic string cleaning, inverter filter swaps, thermal drone scans to identify defective cells, and electrical connection checks.

By deploying solar PV, most enterprises experience a 60% to 80% reduction in monthly grid drawdowns. Typically, capital recovery is achieved in 3 to 4 years, followed by 20+ years of free energy.

Do solar panel systems operate during cloudy or rainy days?

Yes, solar panels work on cloudy or overcast days by converting scattered diffuse sunlight into electricity. However, the output capacity decreases to about 10% to 25% of optimal sunny day ratings. During rainstorms, production drops significantly, but the rain naturally cleans dust from your modules, helping generation rebound when the sun returns.

What is BESS and does Vykon Indus deploy it today?

BESS stands for Battery Energy Storage System. It stores excess daytime solar energy in high-capacity Lithium-Iron-Phosphate (LFP) or Sodium-Ion battery packs, releasing it when solar isn't active or during grid price spikes. Vykon Indus is actively incorporating commercial & industrial BESS system designs as part of our 2026 roadmap. We are currently in the deployment and commissioning phase, with multiple solar hybrid BESS projects underway. Contact us for current availability and system sizing.

What warranties does Vykon Indus offer on installations?

We provide Tier-1 solar modules with a 10-year manufacturer product warranty and a 25-year linear performance warranty (guaranteeing over 80% output at year 25). Central or string inverters come with a standard 5-to-10 year warranty. Additionally, Vykon provides a comprehensive 1-to-2 year execution and workmanship warranty covering structures, cabling, and sealants.

Which states across India does Vykon Indus operate in?

Our core operations and project management team are based in Uttar Pradesh (UP). However, we execute large-scale industrial and utility installations across major energy corridors including Rajasthan, Maharashtra, Gujarat, Haryana, and Delhi.

What is the Vykon Future Lab and how does it benefit commercial clients?

The Vykon Future Lab is our dedicated technology incubator and R&D division. It merges Solar, AI, Robotics, Drones, and Smart Infrastructure under one platform. For our commercial clients, this means access to cutting-edge energy optimization, automated drone-based site assessments, and early integration with next-generation smart microgrids.

What flagship projects are currently under development in the Future Lab?

Our R&D pipeline includes four flagship initiatives: Project V-Sight (AI-driven solar monitoring dashboard), Project AeroScan (drone-assisted automated EPC mapping kits), Project Lumos (predictive solar-grid optimization toolset), and our custom **Modular BESS Prototype** (developed to support hybrid LFP/Sodium-Ion cells).

What is the Airborne DNA Project (Project Origin)?

Project Origin is our long-term bio-energy and grid-sensor research program. By deploying micro-sampling sensors on our inspection drones, we collect and analyze airborne environmental DNA (eDNA). This data is processed via machine learning models to track regional bio-activity, helping us design environmentally integrated solar parks and bio-energy systems.
